Reputation and record are different things. Reputation is what a nameplate did twenty years ago, filtered through everyone who has told you about it since. The record is what its owners filed with the federal regulator, year by year, for the cars actually on sale. When you line the two up, most of the expected names are there — but the list of cars that stay quiet across a decade includes some that never get recommended, and leaves out some that always do.

## The test

For this guide a nameplate qualifies if, across at least eight model years on this site, no single year scores below 50 and the median year scores above 70. That excludes any car with a bad launch year, which rules out most of the market — the Camry, the CR-V, the Accord and the RAV4 all fail on a single year — and leaves the cars that have simply never had a crisis.

## The cars that pass

The GMC Terrain is the surprise. Fifteen model years, none below 72, a median of 79, and two years above 90. It shares the Equinox's 2.4-litre oil-consumption problem in its first generation, but at a level the record barely registers, and its 2018 second generation launched without the spike that nearly every other launch in the dataset shows. Nobody recommends the Terrain. The record does.

The Toyota Corolla passes narrowly: its 2011 year scores 48 on the old engine's oil consumption, but every year from 2012 to 2025 is above 60 and eight of them are above 80. It is the car the reputation says it is, with one caveat at the start of the record and one dip at the 2020 launch.

The Dodge Charger passes from 2015. Its 2011 to 2014 years fail on the electrical-module problem, but the 2015 to 2023 run has no year below 70 and four years at 88 or above — a record no other large rear-wheel-drive car on the site can match, and one that its reputation would never suggest.

The Nissan Rogue passes for 2011 to 2022, with no year below 61 and a median of 70 — the one Nissan of its era whose transmission calibration escaped the worst of the CVT years — before its 2023 engine change ends the run.

The Chevrolet Equinox from 2011 to 2024 has no year below 69 and six years above 80. The Honda Odyssey from 2011 to 2017 has no year below 74 and ends at 92 — and then fails the test entirely from 2018, which is why the test is by year and not by name.

## The cars that do not

The Toyota Camry fails on 2018. The Honda CR-V fails on 2017, 2018 and 2019. The Subaru Outback fails on 2019 and 2020. The Honda Accord fails on 2018. The Mazda CX-5 and the Lexus RX, two cars with strong reputations, do not have deep enough records on this site to qualify — their complaint counts are low, which is good, but low counts mean the confidence label is thin and the score is pulled toward the average. Absence of evidence is not evidence of quality; it is absence of evidence.

## What the list has in common

The cars that pass are, almost without exception, cars that changed slowly. The Terrain and Equinox kept their platform for eight years; the Charger for more than a decade; the Corolla changed generations once in the period; the fourth-generation Odyssey ran for seven years without a new transmission. The cars that fail are the ones that introduced a new turbo engine, a new dual-clutch or nine-speed or ten-speed transmission, or a new electrical architecture — and failed in the year they did it. Dependability in this record is less a property of the manufacturer than of how long the design has been in production.

## How to use this

If you want the lowest-risk used purchase and do not care what it is, buy a 2016 to 2023 Terrain or Equinox, a 2013 to 2019 Corolla, or a 2015 to 2022 Charger, and have it inspected. If you want a specific nameplate that is not on this list, that is fine — nearly every car on the site has good years — but find the good years on its model page before you shop, and read its guide if it has one.

## Summary

The quietest records belong to cars that changed least: Terrain, Corolla, Charger from 2015, Rogue to 2022, Equinox, Odyssey to 2017. The loudest records belong to cars that changed most, in the year they changed.
